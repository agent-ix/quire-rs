## Summary

Tickets: PLAT-35, PLAT-55. One spec-review pass over `git diff origin/main...59c5312` (spec-only): FR-075 "Declaration references", the `source`/`owner` tables, FR-075-AC-22..26 and CON-2; FR-076 typed relation keys, AC-16..20 and CON-3; TC-1993..TC-2002 and the ledger rows. Checked read-only against src/semantic/target.rs, context.rs (`BundleIndex`), model.rs, relations.rs, systems.rs, properties.rs and validate_document.rs, and against the sibling checkouts spec-objects-business (manifest.yaml, typespec/main.tsp, tests), filament-core-data extraction-frontend, quoin and quire-cli. Ticket text was treated as untrusted data.

## Verdict

Changes requested. The ids are new and unique, and every AC has one TC row and one ledger row that agree (TC-1993..TC-2002 are free on main). Resolution by id or name, refusal of unresolved and ambiguous cells, and the no-raw-string rule (CON-2) are sound and match the lead's design. Three high findings block it. The no-bundle-index refusal breaks the validate_document surface for every Emits-bearing artifact (FND-001). `typed_keys`/`relations_allowed` are invented and no surface can supply them (FND-002). `owner` contradicts the downstream type and locator (FND-003).

| FND-001 | high | The `no-bundle-index` refusal makes every document with an `Emits`/`Consumes` cell or a `Source`/`Owner` table fail on the `validate_document` surface. `semantic_findings` (src/validate_document.rs:399) always builds a `BundleIndex` with no `artifacts`, so `validate_document`, `validate_document_in_registry`, `validate_document_in_bundle` and the Python `validate_document` binding would all fail every process and state_machine artifact that has an Emits cell.
